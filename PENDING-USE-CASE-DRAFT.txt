SOMADA PENDING USE-CASE PROPOSAL
Status: FOR DISCUSSION ONLY - NOT YET APPROVED OR APPLIED
Recorded: August 30, 2026

TOPIC
Dean distribution of the official term schedule through SOMADA.

PROPOSED USE CASES

1. Publish Official Term Schedule
   Primary actor: Dean
   Purpose: Allow the Dean to upload or enter the official term schedule and
   publish it to the assigned Faculty members and Head Laboratory.

   Proposed result:
   - SOMADA validates the term, class, and Faculty assignments.
   - The official schedule is stored and versioned.
   - The schedule becomes visible to the assigned Faculty members and Head Laboratory.
   - SOMADA sends a notification when the schedule or a revised version is published.

2. View Assigned Term Schedule
   Primary actor: Faculty
   Purpose: Allow a Faculty member to open and review the official term schedule
   assigned to their classes.

   Note: Automatic receipt of a notification is not a separate use case. This use
   case applies only if Faculty actively opens and reviews the schedule in SOMADA.

PROPOSED WORKFLOW

Dean publishes official term schedule
-> SOMADA validates, stores, and distributes the schedule
-> Faculty views the assigned term schedule
-> Head Laboratory uses the official schedule when establishing laboratory-room
   committed blocks, availability, and vacant blocks.

MODELING NOTES

- "Publish Official Term Schedule" is preferred over "Send Schedule" because it
  describes the Dean's business goal instead of a button action.
- Do not merge "View Assigned Term Schedule" with the current "View Schedule &
  Availability" without confirmation. The first concerns the official academic
  or teaching schedule; the second concerns laboratory-room committed and vacant
  blocks used for reservations.
- Do not add <<include>> or <<extend>> between Publish and View merely because the
  actions occur in sequence or use the same schedule record.
- If approved, the Dean will no longer be described as strictly approval-only.
  The Dean will still remain unable to submit laboratory reservations.
